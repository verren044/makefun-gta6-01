Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built with plain JavaScript and 2D canvas and playable fully offline. The city spans 3600x2800 across five districts — Ember Heights (with its volcano, casino and park), Marina Del Sol, Porto Verde, Old Harbor and Northgate Hills — generated deterministically from a seed, with a road grid, buildings, sidewalks and parks. Twelve drivable cars (the Ember GT tops out at 640) share the streets with 22 traffic cars and 40 pedestrians, while a five-star wanted system sends cop cars and on-foot officers after you when you break the law. An eight-mission story worth $5,500 runs from "First Wheels" to the finale "The Ember Score", with driving, pickups, deliveries, a chase, an escape, a casino shootout and a two-drop heist setup, plus a live minimap, health and money HUD, pause menu and v2 saves.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD/arrows move and drive, E enters/exits a car, J shoots, P pauses, S saves from the pause screen.
